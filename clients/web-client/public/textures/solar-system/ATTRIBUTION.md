# Texture Attribution

All planet, sun, ring and milky-way textures in this folder are from
[Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/),
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Textures are based on NASA elevation and imagery data.

Adaptations made here (permitted by CC BY 4.0):

- `4k_mercury.jpg`, `4k_venus_surface.jpg`, `4k_mars.jpg`, `4k_earth_clouds.jpg`
  are the source 8K maps downscaled to 4096x2048 (JPEG q85) for web delivery.
- `2k_earth_normal_map.png` is the source TIF converted to PNG.
- `2k_earth_roughness_map.png` is the source specular TIF converted to PNG and
  INVERTED (specular water mask -> roughness map: oceans smooth, land rough).
- Uranus and Neptune remain 2K — the source offers no higher resolution.
